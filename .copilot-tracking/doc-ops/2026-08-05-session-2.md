## Requirements

Rename `docs/implementation-guide.md` to
`docs/implementation-guide-internal.md`. Create
`docs/implementation-guide-external.md` as a simpler, customer-facing guide
that explains the recommended enterprise AI Credit governance approach without
requiring this repository's application or any particular assessment tool.

## Discovered Issues

### Pattern Compliance

Nineteen split adjustments were identified: 5 high, 11 medium, and 3 low
severity. The internal and external files need distinct titles, descriptions,
audiences, and scope statements. The external guide should use compact tables,
consistent phase templates, customer-neutral terminology, and approximately
half the internal guide's length. It must exclude internal catalogs, fixed
values, engine terminology, repository links, and temporary rates.

### Accuracy Discrepancies

Twenty-six decision units were reviewed: 6 can be retained, 12 must be
generalized, 6 are internal-only, and 2 should be removed from the external
guide. The reusable concepts are accountable ownership, configurable financial
guardrails, default and exception profiles, controlled pilot and rollout,
reconciliation, exceptions, rollback, and evidence. Provider capabilities,
precedence, enforcement, rates, data availability, and policy inheritance must
be validated by each customer rather than presented as universal behavior.

### Missing Documentation

Fifteen external-guide gaps were identified. The new guide needs explicit
customer scope, a provider-neutral four-layer governance model, a source
register, a baseline worksheet, configurable assignment criteria, a profile
register, seven concise lifecycle phases, alert and escalation controls,
reconciliation, exceptions, rollback, a getting-started checklist, and a short
glossary.

The four portable control layers are:

* Enterprise financial guardrail
* Universal per-user baseline
* Approved higher-usage cohort override
* Organization access-policy archetype, managed separately from budgets

No in-scope documentation currently links to the old filename. One untracked
prompt under `.github/prompts/` references it, and README discoverability would
benefit from links to both guides. Both are outside the requested target scope
and are recorded as follow-up items.
